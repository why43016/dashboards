Methodology

Sales, market share, and fleet stock are from the IEA Global EV Outlook, republished by Our World in Data (CC-BY). "Electric cars" includes battery-electric and plug-in-hybrid passenger cars. Sales growth, cumulative sales, and sales intensity (per 1,000 people, using World Bank population) are derived from those series.

The 2025 vintage is the latest available and may include IEA estimates. Figures are reproduced without adjustment.
