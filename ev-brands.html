Methodology

Definitions differ by manufacturer and are labeled per row. Tesla reports global deliveries (all BEV). BYD reports passenger-car sales split into BEV and PHEV. Volkswagen Group, BMW Group and Mercedes-Benz report BEV deliveries/sales. Mercedes-Benz is shown from 2023 due to a reporting-scope change (Cars division, incl. smart).

Sources: company annual reports and sales releases (BYD, Tesla, Volkswagen AG, BMW AG, Mercedes-Benz AG), compiled from public reporting. "Share of world plugin market" divides each series by global plugin-vehicle sales (BEV+PHEV) from the IEA Global EV Outlook via Our World in Data — an approximation, since the Tesla/VW/BMW/Mercedes series exclude PHEVs.

Figures are reproduced without adjustment; company delivery and registration bases are not identical.
