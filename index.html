Methodology

All series are sourced from the World Bank Open Data API (indicators shown as WB codes) and cover 2000–2024, latest available vintage. Values are reproduced without adjustment. Inflation is the annual change in the consumer price index; unemployment is the ILO-modeled estimate as a share of the labor force. GDP and GDP per capita are in current US dollars.

This dashboard is a demonstration asset built entirely on public data.

About this piece

MACROSCOPE is a portfolio dashboard template: one public dataset, six indicators, twenty-seven economies, zero proprietary data. Built to demonstrate analytical design — reproducible with any open data source.
